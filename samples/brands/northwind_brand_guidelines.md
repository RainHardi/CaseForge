# Northwind Digital Works — Brand Guidelines (fictional, team-made test data)

## Colours
- Primary brand colour: Deep Teal #0E6E73 — headlines, key numbers, charts
- Accent colour: Signal Coral #F2765C — small highlights only, never for body text
- Text colour: Graphite #1C2430
- Background: Paper #FFFFFF
- Surface for cards and panels: Mist #EEF3F3
- Caption grey: #56606B
- Dark panels: Night #0B2B33

## Typography
- Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans, bold
- Body text: Source Serif 4

## Layout
- A thin teal rule along the top of every page
- Logo top-left
- Page numbers in the footer
- Use bar-shaped graphic elements, no hexagons

## Voice
- Write as "we" (Northwind), plain and specific
- Sentences under 22 words
- Don't use "synergy", "best-in-class" or "world-class"
- Sign-off line: "Talk to us about your next project · northwind.example"
