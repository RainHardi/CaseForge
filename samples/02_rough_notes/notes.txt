inventory thing for the noodle factory (fictional - "Han River Noodles")
- they tracked stock in 3 excel files, nobody trusted the numbers
- warehouse guys counted by hand every friday
- we did a dashboard, python + react
- barcode scanning w/ cheap android phones, scans go straight into postgres
- low stock alerts by email when flour or packaging gets low
- biggest pain: their product codes were different in every excel file, took forever to map them
- also wifi in the cold storage room is bad -> scans are queued offline and synced later
- went live in march
- manager said friday count is way faster now
- no hard numbers yet, they didnt measure before
