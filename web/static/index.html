Every sentence shows where it came from.

Generating text is easy. Knowing it is true is the hard part, so the check is built in instead of left to whoever is in a hurry.

  • Written from quoted facts onlyEach fact is pulled from your notes with the exact words and the line it sits on.
  • Checked twiceFixed rules compare numbers, names and estimate wording. A separate AI pass reads each sentence against the notes. The stricter verdict wins.
  • Estimates stay estimates"About 18 minutes" never turns into "18 minutes".
  • When the notes disagree, you decideTwo different numbers for the same thing become a question for you, not a guess.

On our own test set the checker caught 60 of 60 planted errors and raised no false flags on 109 true sentences.

Four steps. Only one needs you.

You bring the notes and the final say. Everything between is done for you.

  1. Add your notes

    Upload PDF, Word, Excel or PowerPoint files, or paste rough notes and chat threads. Add your logo if you have one.

  2. Get a checked draft

    CaseForge pulls out the facts, writes from those facts alone, and marks anything the notes don't back in red, with the reason.

  3. You approve

    Read it, fix or delete what is red, and approve. Your name and the date go on the document. Any later edit removes the approval.

  4. Download the report

    The report is laid out for you the moment you approve. Change anything by typing, approve the layout, and download.

A report laid out from what your notes contain.

Three to five pages. The layout is chosen from the content: projects with measured results lead with numbers, projects with a strong client quote lead with the quote.

Numbers get room

Key results are set large. Before-and-after pairs become a chart.

Each fact once

A number on the cover is not repeated inside. Short copy, no walls of text.

Your logo sets the colours

Upload a logo and the report takes its colours from it. No logo: a palette matched to the subject of your notes.

Checked again

Every headline, label and number in the layout is checked against the notes a second time before you can approve it.

Change it by asking.

There are no templates to pick from. Type what you want, in your own words: the layout, the graphs, the colours, the order, the wording. New wording is checked like everything else.

Why not just ask a chatbot?

We tested a general AI tool on the same messy notes six times. It invented no numbers, but added four to six claims per draft that the notes never made.

A general AI chat

  • Writes fluently, fills gaps with plausible guesses
  • Leaves checking to you, sentence by sentence
  • Nothing stops an unchecked draft from being sent
  • No record of what came from where

CaseForge

  • Writes only from facts quoted from your notes
  • Flags anything the notes don't back, with the reason
  • Download stays locked until a person approves
  • Every sentence traces back to its source line
